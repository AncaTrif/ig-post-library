This stew reminds me so much of home 🐓 It is how my mom sometimes cooks rice with chicken. I used as many ingredients from Romania as I could: chicken fat (schmalz), dill and parsley from her garden (I keep them frozen) and her zarzavat, a delicious mix of jarred veggies that people back home keep in their pantries for thicker soups and stews.

Young rooster stew with zucchini, risotto rice & dill
serves 4 portions · 75 to 90 min 

You need:
1 kg young rooster (or chicken), in bone-in pieces
300 g diced zucchini (mine was frozen)
180 g risotto rice
100 g onion, chopped
30 g chicken schmalz
1/2 jar zarzavat (or Gemüsebrühe or any substitute)
1150 ml hot water
a generous handful each of dill and parsley
8 g salt, white pepper
1/2 to 1 lemon

How to:
1. Melt the schmalz, sweat the onion for 5 min, add the rooster and brown lightly.
2. Add 800 ml hot water, zarzavat (jarred veggies), salt and pepper. Cover and simmer gently for 35 to 45 min, until tender.
3. Add the zucchini and simmer 5 to 8 min.
4. Add the rice and 350 ml hot water. Cover and simmer 18 to 20 min, adding more hot water if needed.
5. Stir in the dill and parsley, then add lemon juice bit by bit and adjust the salt.

It should come out moist, loose and slightly creamy. Next day, add 100 to 200 ml water when reheating, because the rice keeps drinking 🤍
