I've been painting women who cook lately, as I celebrate my grandma, my mom, and all the women out there who are not afraid to experiment and make something out of almost nothing, for themselves or for their families. 🍲

Cooking can break your body, but it will never crush your spirit. On the contrary, it will elevate you and enmesh you in smells, textures and colors. It is a multidimensional art that involves smell, vision and even sound: the crackling of the fire in the stove, the wooden planks under your feet as you move around the counter, the sizzling in the pan. All the colors, all the smells.

I celebrate women who cook (and not just women) to survive, to dream, to try things out, to feed, to fulfil a promise, whatever call they have.
