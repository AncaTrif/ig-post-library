Pumpkin season is back in my Friedrichshain kitchen, and the pumpkins are in a great mood.

This time they were roasted with sage and red onion, baked onto a flatbread with anchovies, and stirred into a big casserole with chickpeas for the table.

I promise you the taste of this will stay with you for months.
