I lost my train of thought eating this pie 🥧 (you may have noticed in the second photo).

I have to be honest with you: the secret to how delicious it is lives mostly in the lard in the dough. It makes the crust tender, nutty and old-fashioned in the best way.

Here is how you make it, one tray (30 x 38 cm):

FILLING
Peel and coarsely grate 14 apples (about 1.4 kg). Cook them with 15 g sugar for 10 to 15 minutes until soft and juicy. Stir in 2 very ripe mashed bananas at the end, plus 1 tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp freshly grated ginger, ⅛ tsp cardamom, a tiny pinch of fennel and black pepper, the zest of ½ lemon, 1 tbsp lemon juice and a pinch of salt.

DOUGH
Mix 500 g spelt flour (type 630), 90 g sugar, 15 g vanilla sugar, 10 g baking powder and a pinch of salt. Rub in 175 g cold lard until it looks like coarse breadcrumbs. Beat 1 egg with 80 g cream, add it, and bring everything together gently, because spelt likes a light hand. Split the dough 60:40 and chill for 30 minutes.

ASSEMBLE & BAKE
Roll out the bigger part to 3 to 4 mm and line the tray. Spread the filling on top, cover with the rest of the dough, press the edges together and prick the top with a fork. Bake at 180°C (top and bottom heat) for about 40 minutes, until golden.

Let it cool for at least 45 minutes before cutting. It is just as lovely completely cold. 🤍
