What to do with some pork chops, some Hungarian paprika powder (spicy) and some frozen zucchini from my mom in Romania? 🐖🌶️

A rich Hungarian-inspired pork stew with potatoes and tomato, served with autumn pasta and grandma's spicy pickled peppers on the side. My Hungarian partner loved it, and we are still debating whether it counts as pörkölt, so for now it stays "inspired".

4 portions · about 1 hour 15 min · Dutch oven

You need:
600 g pork chops, cut into 3 cm pieces
50 g pork fat, diced small (or 2 to 3 tbsp lard)
100 g onion, chopped
4 garlic cloves
500 g potatoes, in 3 cm cubes
20 g tomato paste
400 ml water, plus hot water as needed
3/4 tsp hot (Hungarian) paprika
1/2 to 1 tsp marjoram
1/2 tsp crushed black pepper
4 to 5 g salt to start, adjust at the end
250 g autumn pasta
pickled hot peppers and a splash of pickle brine, to serve

How to:
1. Render the pork fat in the Dutch oven for 4 to 6 min and keep 2 to 3 tbsp.
2. Brown the pork in batches and set it aside.
3. Cook the onion for about 8 min until soft and golden, then add the garlic for 1 min.
4. Off the heat, stir in the paprika, marjoram, pepper and tomato paste, and add the water right away.
5. Return the pork, cover and simmer for 25 to 30 min. Add the potatoes and simmer 20 to 25 min more, until everything is tender.
6. Cook the pasta as the pack says and save a little pasta water.
7. Taste and adjust. Pasta water gives the sauce more body, and 1 to 2 tsp pickle brine adds acidity if it needs it.

Serve with the pasta and the pickled peppers on the side 🤍
