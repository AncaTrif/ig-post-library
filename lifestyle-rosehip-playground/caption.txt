Going almost every day to the playground with my daughter means that I will always see these wonderful colors of autumn shrubs and hedges. If I did not have my daughter I would miss a million details from nature, from the streets, from the world. In those hours of moving and playing around, I get to hide and seek in hidden places moist from last night's rain. I see the twigs fall in different directions, their dry fruit hanging beautifully like in a master's paintings, and fade from green into reds and pinks, such splendors. I imagine them on a table, in a vase, somewhere I can admire them at home, but then remember that their purpose is to stay outside over there, so that we make the effort to see them, touch them, admire them.
