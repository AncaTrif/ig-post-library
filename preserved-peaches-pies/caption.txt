Peach, cardamom & lemon turnovers 🍑 

I used ready-rolled Blätterteig (puff pastry), preserved peaches, a pinch of cardamom and lemon zest. No extra sugar needed.

The recipe in large strokes (makes 8-10, 15 min prep):
• Drain 500 g preserved peaches, cut small, pat dry (replace with fresh fruit if you have some or any other preserved fruit around the pantry)
• Mix with ¼ tsp cardamom, zest of ½ lemon, a pinch of salt and 3 tbsp Semmelbrösel (breadcrumbs)
• Cut the puff pastry into 8-10 squares, put a modest spoonful of fruit filling on one half, fold, seal 
• Brush with egg or milk, cut a small slit for the steam
• Bake at 200°C Ober-/Unterhitze for 18-25 minutes, until puffed and deep golden

Let them rest 5-10 minutes on the tray. Your filling is too juicy? Use the extra spoon of breadcrumbs.

It turned out crisp, buttery, flaky, with a fragrant peach filling, floral cardamom and a fresh lemon finish.
