Mung Bean Soup 🍲
Cold weather calls for a soup that feels like a balm for the soul. This one is cosy, warming and, if you ask it to be, properly spicy.

🧾 YOU NEED
Mung beans, fresh ginger, garam masala and turmeric.

🌶️ THE SPICE MIX
Garlic, cumin, mustard seeds and chili powder. Add as much chili as you dare and the soup turns up the heat with you.
