In the countryside, my mom bakes aubergines on an old yard stove, set on logs, with real fire and real heat. I should know, I burned myself a couple of times on it. 🍆🔥

But that smell, the smell of baked aubergine, is one of the 5 reasons to be alive. Just kidding, there are more, but still, the aubergine must have been sent down by the gods, with a sneer.

Making "aubergine salad" as we call it in Romania is a wonderful experience and, at the end, a delicious treat. So much so that I am adding the recipe to the small recipe booklet I am painting and curating right now. Watch this space! 📖
