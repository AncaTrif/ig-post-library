Have you ever wondered how autumn has been revered since time immemorial, and yet how much we underestimate her in today's culture? 🍂

We look forward to summer and warmth and sunny days, yet we forget how incredibly strong this season is and how much she brings. Especially in places like Berlin, where it gets cold and dark so quickly, her powers, her colors, her richness in texture and smell have been overlooked. I want to bring her back to the feast. 🎃
