Dark chocolate, banana and Hokkaido pumpkin cake, with lots of toasted coconut on top. 🍫

It's baked in a 28 cm tin with no added sugar, so it's bitter-sweet. If you want it rounder, add 90 to 100 g sugar.

What goes in:
250 g ripe banana
350 g cooked Hokkaido purée (about 500 g raw, roasted with the skin on until soft, then blended)
120 g coconut fat, melted and cooled
300 g flour
55 g cocoa
80 g coconut flakes (60 g in the batter, 20 g on top, toasted in a dry pan)
3 eggs, 3 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp vanilla, ½ tsp salt
60 g dark chocolate (70 to 85%), chopped

How it went: whisk the banana, pumpkin, eggs, fat and vanilla. Mix the flour, cocoa, baking powder and salt, then fold everything together with the coconut and chocolate. The batter is thick and won't level itself, so spread it with a spatula and scatter the coconut on top. Bake at 170°C for 45 to 55 minutes, until a skewer comes out with moist crumbs. Let it rest in the tin for 15 to 20 minutes.

My own little quirk: I spread peanut butter and Romanian sour cherry jam, because the crumb is a bit dense and wants something creamy. Vanilla whipped cream works too.
