Growing my own sourdough starter from nothing but rye flour and water. 20 g of flour, 20 ml of water, the warmest spot in the house, and a lot of giddy waiting. Some days it bubbles, some jars lag behind, and I keep going back to feed it until the culture grows stronger. Somehow, this daily ritual has become truly soul-nourishing. 🌾

Have you ever grown your own starter? Tell me how it went in the comments!
