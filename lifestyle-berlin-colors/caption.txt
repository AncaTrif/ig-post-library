Berlin in autumn is gray. But there is something that saves it, something that goes beyond shitty weather, and that is splashes of color that no other city allows to shine through like this one. I love a splash of neon pink or a dash of neon orange just around the corner, just when you were deciding to go and curl in a ball at home. The splash triggers something like a jolt, almost like a little shock of joy. It's ok, these colors say, we know how this feels, and here is contrast, here is something funky and shiny and in your eyes, even if it's an ad, a shop, a cafe. The splash invites you to stay outside, to continue to push through the drizzle, to stray along, don't go yet, linger.
