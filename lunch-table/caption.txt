Ten hours in the car to the east, and every kilometre is worth it.

Home-made sausages, fruit from the gardens we grew up in, jars of preserves that overflow our little cellar and our kitchen shelves.

It took me a while, maybe a little late, to understand that family and community are where I draw my energy from. Two months away from Berlin charge my batteries for the whole year.

The star of this lunch is the Hungarian Kolbász. I fried it briefly in olive oil and added it to our veggie bowl, next to grapes from the garden.
