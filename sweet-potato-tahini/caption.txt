Tahini Sweet Potato Côtelette 🍠
Roasted sweet potatoes, quinoa and a silky tahini dressing, with a juicy oven-roasted pork côtelette, apple and onion. Serves 6-8.

🧾 YOU NEED

Bowl: 1.5-2 kg sweet potatoes, 2 cups quinoa, 3-4 green onions, 1-2 apples, 4-5 tbsp olive oil, 1½ tsp sweet paprika, 1 tbsp za'atar, 2-3 tbsp toasted sesame seeds, salt and pepper

Dressing: 6-8 tbsp tahini, juice of 1 lemon, 1½ tsp salt, 4-6 tbsp warm water, 1 small garlic clove (optional)

Pork: 1 bone-in côtelette (1.2-1.5 kg), salt, pepper, paprika, cumin, oregano, 2-3 tbsp olive oil, 1 apple, 1 onion, a little honey or cider vinegar (optional)

👩‍🍳 HOW

1. The night before: pat the pork dry, rub with 1½ tsp salt per kg, pepper and a pinch of sugar. Leave it uncovered in the fridge for 8-24 h, then rest 1 h at room temperature.
2. Toss the diced sweet potatoes with oil, paprika, za'atar, salt and pepper. Roast at 200 °C for 25-30 min, turning halfway.
3. Simmer the rinsed quinoa (2 cups in 4 cups water) for 15 min, rest 5 min, fluff.
4. Whisk the dressing until smooth and pourable.
5. Sear the pork 3-4 min per side in a hot pan, add the apple and onion for 1-2 min, then move everything with the pan juices into a Dutch oven.
6. Season with paprika, cumin and oregano, tent with foil and roast at 190 °C for 25-35 min, until 63 °C inside. Foil off for the last 10 min, then rest 10 min.
7. Toss quinoa, sweet potatoes, green onions and apple with the dressing and top with sesame and za'atar. Slice the pork across the grain and serve with the roasted apple, onion and extra dressing.
