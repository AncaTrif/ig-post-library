# Roasted Sweet Potato & Tahini Grain Bowl with Oven-Roasted Pork Côtelette (6-8 servings)

Full recipe as written by Anca. The caption holds a shortened version because Instagram captions are limited to 2,200 characters.

## Ingredients

### Grain Bowl
- Sweet potatoes: 1.5-2 kg (5-6 medium), diced
- Quinoa: 2 cups uncooked (about 380 g)
- Green onions: 3-4 stalks, chopped (optional)
- Apple: 1-2, thinly sliced (optional)
- Olive oil: 4-5 tbsp
- Sweet paprika: 1½ tsp
- Za'atar: 1 tbsp + extra for topping
- Salt & black pepper: to taste
- Sesame seeds: 2-3 tbsp, toasted

### Tahini Dressing
- Tahini: 6-8 tbsp (100-120 g)
- Lemon juice: 2-3 tbsp (1 lemon)
- Salt: 1½ tsp (adjust)
- Warm water: 4-6 tbsp (to thin)
- Garlic: 1 small clove, grated (optional)

### Pork Côtelette
- 1 large bone-in pork côtelette (about 1.2-1.5 kg)
- Salt: 1½ tsp per kg (for dry brine)
- Black pepper, paprika, cumin, oregano
- Olive oil: 2-3 tbsp for searing
- 1 apple + 1 onion, sliced
- (Optional) 1 tsp honey or splash of apple-cider vinegar

## Directions

### 1. Dry-Brine (Overnight)
1. Pat the pork dry.
2. Rub with 1½ tsp salt per kg, pepper, and a pinch of sugar.
3. Place on a rack over a plate, uncovered 8-24 h in the fridge.
4. Before cooking, let sit 1 h at room temperature, then pat dry again.

This draws in seasoning and keeps the meat juicy.

### 2. Roast the Sweet Potatoes
1. Preheat oven to 200 °C / 400 °F.
2. Toss cubes with oil, paprika, za'atar, salt, pepper.
3. Roast 25-30 min, flipping halfway.

### 3. Cook the Quinoa
Rinse well, boil 2 cups quinoa + 4 cups water/broth, simmer 15 min, rest 5 min, fluff.

### 4. Make the Tahini Dressing
Whisk tahini + lemon juice + salt + water until smooth and pourable. Add garlic if you like.

### 5. Sear the Pork (in Stainless Pan)
1. Heat 2-3 tbsp oil in a stainless-steel skillet until shimmering.
2. Lower the pork gently (meat side first).
3. Sear 3-4 min per side until golden.
4. Add the apple and onion slices to the same pan for 1-2 min to pick up color and flavor.
5. Transfer everything (pork + fruit + onion + all juices) into your Dutch oven.

Include all pan juices, they contain the fond that adds depth to the roast.

### 6. Roast the Pork (Option A: Crisp & Juicy)
1. Sprinkle on paprika, cumin, oregano, and a touch of honey or vinegar if using.
2. Drizzle a bit of olive oil over the top.
3. Tent loosely with foil (leave a small gap for airflow).
4. Roast 190 °C / 375 °F for 25-35 min, until internal temp = 63 °C (145 °F).
5. Remove foil for last 10 min for color.
6. Rest 10 min before slicing.

### 7. Assemble the Grain Bowl
Combine quinoa + roasted sweet potatoes + green onions + apple. Toss with tahini dressing. Top with sesame and extra za'atar.

### 8. Serve
Slice the pork across the grain. Serve over or beside the grain bowl. Drizzle with some tahini dressing and add roasted apple-onion from the pot.
