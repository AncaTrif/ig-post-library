Full disclosure: I forgot to unfreeze the pork chops. Under time pressure, they went into the pot frozen, and the stew turned out amazing anyway 🥶🍲

Red wine pork & black lentil stew, all in one pot:
pork chops, Beluga lentils, black beans, potatoes, cauliflower, onions, garlic, rosemary, thyme and a good splash of red wine. Everything bakes in a Dutch oven at 180°C for about 80 to 90 minutes, then a few spoons of crème fraîche go on top at the end, if you will.

Pork should reach 63°C inside, and frozen or thick chops may need longer. The black beans are cooked separately first.
