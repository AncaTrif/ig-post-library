Red Wine Pork & Black Lentil Stew
A rustic, one-pot autumn stew with rosemary, thyme, red wine and a creamy finish.

Ingredients
• 4 pork chops, frozen
• 400 g Beluga lentils
• 400 g dried black beans, soaked
• 3 medium potatoes, diced
• 250 g cauliflower, in florets
• Onions and garlic
• 100-150 ml red wine
• Butter
• Salt and black pepper
• Rosemary and thyme
• Water or broth
• 2-3 tbsp crème fraîche

Method
1. Prepare the beans: Drain the soaked black beans, cover with fresh water, bring to a rolling boil for at least 30 minutes, then continue boiling until completely tender. Drain and set aside. Some beans need longer.
2. Assemble the stew: Place the frozen pork chops in a Dutch oven. Add the rinsed Beluga lentils, onions, garlic, butter, rosemary, thyme, salt, pepper and red wine. Add hot water or broth until the lentils are submerged.
3. Start cooking: Cover and bake at 180°C conventional heat for 30 minutes.
4. Add the potatoes: Nestle the diced potatoes into the liquid. Cover and bake for another 20 minutes.
5. Add the cauliflower: Stir in the cauliflower and add more hot water if needed. Cover and bake for another 20 minutes.
6. Finish the stew: Add the cooked black beans and bake for another 10-15 minutes, until heated through. Check that the lentils and potatoes are tender and that the pork reaches at least 63°C internally, followed by a 3-minute rest. Frozen or thick chops may need longer.
7. Make it creamy: Stir in the crème fraîche, adjust the seasoning and serve.

Cooking time: approximately 80-90 minutes in the oven, plus the time needed to prepare the beans. Allow extra time if the pork chops are particularly thick or frozen together.

A hearty, wine-rich stew for chilly days, with minimal hands-on cooking.
